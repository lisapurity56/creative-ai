## image generation prompt design.

i.
Basic/Vague Prompt

Prompt:

A futuristic city skyline at sunset.

Expected Result:

The AI will generate a city skyline with futuristic architecture and a sunset backdrop.

The style, colors, and perspective may vary unpredictably.

Could be realistic, cartoonish, or abstract depending on the model’s default bias.

Problem:

Too open-ended → inconsistent results.

Hard to control lighting, mood, or art style.

Not ideal if you want a specific visual aesthetic.

ii.
Refined Prompt With Constraints

Prompt:

A futuristic city skyline at sunset, painted in watercolor style with warm orange and purple hues.
Include tall glass skyscrapers with neon lights, flying cars, and reflections on a river in the foreground.
Perspective: slightly elevated, looking diagonally across the city.
Mood: dreamy and serene.

Features Added:

Art style: watercolor

Colors: warm oranges and purples

Details: skyscrapers, neon lights, flying cars, river reflections

Perspective: slightly elevated diagonal view

Mood: dreamy, serene

iii. 
| Aspect         | Vague Prompt            | Refined Prompt                              |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Art Style      | Random or default       | Watercolor                                  |
| Colors         | May vary                | Warm orange & purple                        |
| Details        | Minimal or inconsistent | Skyscrapers, flying cars, river reflections |
| Perspective    | Random                  | Slightly elevated diagonal view             |
| Mood           | Ambiguous               | Dreamy & serene                             |
| Predictability | Low                     | High                                        |


iv .
Best Practices for Image Generation Prompts

Specify art style – watercolor, oil painting, photorealistic, 3D render.

Include colors or lighting – sets mood and tone.

Add key objects/details – ensures important elements appear.

Mention perspective/angle – aerial view, close-up, wide shot.

Define mood/emotion – whimsical, dramatic, serene.

Avoid vague adjectives alone – e.g., “cool” or “nice” is too subjective.
